**The editor `edit`.** `start("edit")` opens a window that edits one text of its private store, with no modes and no save action: a character goes in at the caret, Shift with the arrows, Home, End or the pages selects, the pointer places the caret and selects with a drag, a long line goes on more rows, and a gutter numbers the lines. Each word, line end, or change before a move to another line is one commit, so Ctrl+Z restores the version before from the store, also after a restart, and Ctrl+Y goes forward again. A text holds at most 32768 bytes, and an invalid sequence from the store shows as the replacement character. The clipboard, Ctrl+F and the edit of another object are not built yet.
