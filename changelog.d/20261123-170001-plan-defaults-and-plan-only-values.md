**Plan defaults and plan-only values.** A plan of principal 0 gives values to the owners of settings: a default under the choice of the principal, or a lock over it, for the settings of `settings` and of the display, and the values that only the plan holds: the retention rule of the versions, the quota of a store, the share of the machine for one process and the sizes of the screen of memory. Each owner reads them at its next start, and each change of one asks the consent of the principal on the trusted display.
