**`describe(name)` explains one name**: a command gives its signature and its help, and a word of Lex (`let`, `in`, `with`, `|>`, `_`, `=>`, `if`) or `map`, `filter`, `where` and `connect` give a short guide with an example. `help()` stays the table of the commands, and `help("let")` now says to use `describe("let")`.
