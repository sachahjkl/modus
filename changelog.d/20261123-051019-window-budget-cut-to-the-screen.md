type: Changed

**The budget of a resizable window counts no more than the screen**: a module now states the least size of a resizable window in its budget, as the constants that it passes to `display.resizable_set`, and the build refuses a budget whose least size differs from that call. When that least size fits 960 x 540, the logical screen at the scale 2, the tool counts each picture of the window at its largest size at the scale 2, cut on each side to the screen of 1920 x 1080, because the desktop fits such a window into the screen before a scale applies; every other window is counted with no cut, as a window that is not resizable keeps its size at each scale. A resizable window of 1536 x 864 now asks for 4 050 pages of pictures in place of 10 368, and `paint` and `clock` fit again under the largest budget of a process.
