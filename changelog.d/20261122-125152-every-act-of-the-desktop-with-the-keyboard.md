**Every act of the desktop with the keyboard.** Control+Shift+F7 moves the window in front with the arrow keys and Control+Shift+F8 resizes it, by 16 logical pixels or by 1 with Shift: Enter keeps the result, and Escape gives back the old place and size; the palette offers the same as "move window" and "resize window". Control+Shift+F10 gives the bar the focus of the keys: Left and Right reach the heron button, the button of the windows, each window plate and the viewer mark, Down and Up walk the lines of the start menu and of the menu of the windows, Enter acts and Escape returns to the window. The element with the focus in a window, the item of the bar and the window that the arrows hold now show a 2 pixel outline in the strong text outside them, whose contrast a host test computes at 7.3 to 1 or more on each surface of the dark, light and two high contrast sets.
