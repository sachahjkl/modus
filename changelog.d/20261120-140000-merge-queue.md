**A merge queue by batches.** `modustool queue <branch>...` merges a batch of branches into `master` after one full check of the whole batch, each branch with its own merge commit, then pushes `master` and removes the merged branches and their worktrees. When the check fails, it checks the halves of the batch to name the branch at fault and merges the halves that pass: one branch at fault costs at most 1 + 2⌈log₂ n⌉ full checks, 9 for 12 branches, and a batch never passes its budget of min(2n − 1, 1 + 4⌈log₂ n⌉) full checks.
