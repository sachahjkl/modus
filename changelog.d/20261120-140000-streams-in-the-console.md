**Record streams in the console**: a line names a record output or a record input of a process by its typed name, `output_counter.counts`, and reads a record output as a value of the kind `Stream`: `where` checks a condition on each record, and its first condition goes with the wiring so the writer writes only the records that pass it, and the console refuses a record that fails it as a fault of the writer; `columns`, `first(s, n)` and `table(s)` work on it, `stream_counts(s)` gives the counts that the writer published, and a `Stream` at the end of a line draws each row as it comes, with its joined record and its gaps, until its end or Escape. `wire(from, to)` gives a row of a wiring list, `+` joins two lists, and `start(module, list)` starts a module with its wirings made before its first instruction. `record_outputs(module)` lists the record outputs of a module.
