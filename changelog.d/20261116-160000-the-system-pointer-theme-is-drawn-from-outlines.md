**The pointer theme of the system has smooth edges.** Its 15 shapes are outlines that the build draws with exact coverage at 24 and 48 pixels, in place of grids of squares: a white fill inside a black edge of 1 pixel, with each level and upright edge on whole pixels. `wait` and `busy_with_arrow` turn a dot around a ring in 8 frames of 125 ms. A pointer manifest takes the statements `layer`, `contour`, `line`, `curve` and `circle`, and no longer takes lines of a grid.
