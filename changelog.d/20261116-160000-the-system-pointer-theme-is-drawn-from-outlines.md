**The pointer theme of the system has smooth edges, two forms and accents.** Its 15 shapes are outlines that the build draws with exact coverage at 24 and 48 pixels, in place of grids of squares: a fill inside an edge of 1 pixel at both sizes, with each level and upright edge on whole pixels. The dark form is white in a dark edge, the light form dark in a white edge; the compositor draws the dark form until the settings say that the light appearance is in force. The dot that turns in `wait` and `busy_with_arrow` and the question mark of `help` are in the blue of the focus, the slash of `forbidden` in the red of "stop a program", the dot of `crosshair` in amber. A pointer theme file has the version 2, with a form count, and a pointer manifest names its layers by role and its colors by token.
