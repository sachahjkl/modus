**The facts of the other system modules.** The debugger, the start policy, the module file service, the date service, the call host, the windows `boot` and `pages`, `assemble` and `c_compiler` write their lines as log facts, and so do the lines that the services of the devices and of the network, `remote` and `remote_display` still wrote as text: the stop of a service with its failure, the public keys and the listen ports of `remote` and `remote_display`, their measures, and the block device that is ready. A reader filters them by field, for example `log.records(0) |> facts(debugger.Thread_Stopped) |> where(thread = 0)`. Each sentence is the line of before, and the lines with a number in hexadecimal, a status written as its number, a free sentence of an error record, no value, or more than 144 bytes of values stay text.
