**The facts of the other system modules.** The debugger, the start policy, the module file service, the date service, the call host, the windows `boot` and `pages`, `assemble` and `c_compiler` write their lines as log facts, and so do the lines that the TPM, storage, sound, entropy, frame, block, input and network services, `remote` and `remote_display` still wrote as text: the stop of a service with its failure, the lines with no value, the response codes of the TPM, the public keys and the listen ports, the measures, and one fact for each fault of a debug object. A reader filters them by field, for example `log.records(0) |> facts(debugger.Thread_Stopped) |> where(thread = 0)`. Each sentence is the line of before, but for three forms: a status of the kernel is written by the name of its member (`status No_Memory`, not `status 4`), a refusal of the writer of module files in `assemble` too (`refusal Name_Twice`, not `refusal 3`), and a refused request of `remote`, a refusal of the block service and a refusal of the store of `module_files` write the values of their error record (`kind Too_Small, unit Bytes, operation 0, argument 0, value 20, limit 104, found 0`) in place of its sentence. A line of the diagnostic of `c_compiler` longer than 143 bytes is one fact for each piece of 143 bytes. Three lines stay text: the two lines of `module_files` with a request path of up to 200 bytes, and the load error of the call host.
