**Text with no size**: the editor `edit` holds a text of the size that its budget pays, in place of 32 KiB, and reads and writes it by ranges, so a log of 100 KB or a long source opens whole. A line of the console holds 4096 bytes in place of 200, with 256 levels of nesting, 4096 items in its lists and a history of 1024 lines kept across a stop; a variable keeps its value in memory of its size, and the console keeps 64 of them. The clipboard holds a copy of the size that the process that copies pays, in place of 4096 bytes, and the place that pastes takes what its room holds.
