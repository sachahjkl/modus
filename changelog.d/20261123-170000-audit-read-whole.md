**An audit space is read whole, and its collector starts with a few reads.** A reader of an audit space now reads each record that it keeps, the oldest ones from the store, not only the newest 64. A collector reads a few objects for each space at its start, whatever its count of records, and its start line gives that count. The memory of the collector no longer limits the bounds of its spaces: the act spaces of a wide grant and several audits of servers fit at once.
