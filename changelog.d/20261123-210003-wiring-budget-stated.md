**The wiring budget of a process is stated, and pays its entries.** A module states the pages of its wiring rings in its budget (`wiring_pages`); the root service gives it that amount at its first wiring, or half of its pages when it states none, whatever it spent before. The entries that its wirings take in the wiring service are paid from it, so one process cannot fill the wiring service for the others.
