**The root service writes log facts**: the start and the stop of a process, a connection made or ended, a grant revoked or given to debug, and the start of a device service are records of fields, whose sentences are those of before. In the console, `log.records(0) |> facts(root.Process_Stopped) |> where(outcome = Done)` gives the clean stops of the processes, with the stop cause and the stop outcome as columns, and the window of the log writes each sentence. The two new contract enums `Stop_Cause` and `Stop_Outcome` name the members in a condition; a sentence writes the words of a stop, "itself, done".
