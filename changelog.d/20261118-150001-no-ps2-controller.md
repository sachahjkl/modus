**No PS/2 controller.** On a machine with no PS/2 controller the input service no longer reads its status port for ever on one processor: a status of all ones, or a drain of more than 100 000 bytes, gives `input: no PS/2 controller: no keyboard and no mouse` and a stop with the code 1, and the desktop runs with no keyboard and no mouse. A port or an interrupt of the controller that the kernel refuses gives one line of the root service in place of a failed start.
