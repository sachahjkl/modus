**The windows of notes, inspect and log take their places from a layout tree.** Each frame lays out their parts at the size that the window has then: the lines and the action bar of notes, the list of the processes, the address space, the modules, the threads and the bytes of inspect, and the counts and the table of the records of log. The tables take their columns from the layout, and the colors come from the element tokens of the appearance in force. At their size of start the three windows look as before.
