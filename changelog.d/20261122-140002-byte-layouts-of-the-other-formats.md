**Byte layouts of the other formats.** The guides show the record of a ring, the error record, the context, the log record, the fault ring and each block of the data disk byte by byte, from the offsets of the compiler. A field of more than 8 rows, such as the 480 rows of the stores of the root block, is drawn folded, with the offsets of its first and its last row.
