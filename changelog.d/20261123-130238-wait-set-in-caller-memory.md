**A wait set in the memory of the caller.** A `system.Wait` holds 64 words on the stack of its thread, about 3.5 KiB in place of 12 KiB, and a thread that waits on more sizes a `system.Wait_Set` to its tables, in its own memory. The kernel holds the words past 64 in a wait room of the thread, which the budget of its process pays, up to 65536 words: a new command `Thread_Control_Command.Wait_Room` gives the calling thread its room, and the library asks for it at the first wait with a set. A wait holds 64 words with no room, in place of 256. A word past the room is refused: the wait then ends only at its deadline, with one line on the log, and no loop turns with no wait. The compositor waits on each of its 128 windows, and the root service on each queue of each of its places, so it refuses no start for its wait.
