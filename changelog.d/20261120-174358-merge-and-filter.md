**Utility modules: merge and filter**: `merge` joins several record streams of one record into one, and `filter` keeps the records of a stream that pass one condition; each works on a stream of any record, whose record the first wiring fixes and the root service checks for each later source. In the console, `start("merge", wire(a, merge.sources) + wire(b, merge.sources))` then `merge.merged` reads the joined stream.
