**The test modules write log facts**: each line of the test modules is a record of fields, but the record of `log_reader` and of its children, whose text the test reads back, the two lines that measure the write of a text, the long text of `probe`, and three lines that only a copy of `visitor` built outside the sources writes. Each case of `hierarchy` and `ranges` is a fact type of its own whose last field is its verdict, so a case that fails now ends with "WRONG" (`hierarchy: a tree cannot move inside itself: WRONG`), and `where(verdict = "WRONG")` keeps the failed cases. A status of the kernel and an error kind that a line wrote as a number are now the names of their members (`debug_target: the second thread did not start: status No_Slot`). In the console, `log.records(0) |> facts(ranges.Loop_Commits_Made)` gives the time of each fifty commits of range writes.
