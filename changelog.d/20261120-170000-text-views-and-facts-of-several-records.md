**Text views and facts of several records**: a log fact holds texts of any length up to 1 KiB as text views of 16 bytes (`value_kinds.Text_View`), each with its length and its first bytes in the record and the rest in the out-of-line area of the fact, which takes up to 9 records in a row and one line on the serial port; a reader that loses a record of a fact shows its records raw with a mark, never a wrong sentence, and `where` compares a view by its length and its start first. A load error is a fact of one shape for each stage (`module.Load_Find_Fields` and the others). New field kinds write tenths (`12.3`), an address with no leading zero (`0x1a2b`) and a time of day (`07:04`), and a status of `operation.Object_Kind`, `module.Load_Stage`, `module.Error`, `module.Difference`, `block.Status`, `storage_status.Status`, `call_status.Call_Status` and the problems of a pointer theme is written by the name of its member, each also in a word type of 8 bytes; `storage.Status` and `system.Call_Status` moved into the contract packages `storage_status` and `call_status`.
