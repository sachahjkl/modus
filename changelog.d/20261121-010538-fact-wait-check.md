**A check of the waits of the scenarios before any machine runs.** After its build, `modustool check without_machines` and `modustool test` hold each text of the log that a step waits for, and that starts with the prefix of a fact template, against the fact templates of the module files and the text lines of the system; a fact whose sentence changed, a fact split into two or a list that became one fact for each row fails there with its file, its line and the nearest template, not at the hang bound of a machine. `modustool fact_waits` runs it alone; the 1879 fact waits of the scenarios pass it.
