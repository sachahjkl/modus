**A window that scrolls when it does not fit the work area.** Each window gets at most the work area, at each scale and from its first picture, and keeps its layout: a window whose layout is larger scrolls it, with a scroll bar on each side that does not fit, the wheel, Page Up and Page Down, and the view moving to the control that Tab reaches. A window that the work area cut gets its size back when the work area grows. Settings opened at 200 % fits the screen, and Shift+Tab reaches its last control.
