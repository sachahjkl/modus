**Larger guests and guest disks.** A guest process takes up to 32 guest processors. A guest build takes up to 16 GiB of guest memory, and input and output disks of up to 1 GiB with up to 4096 files, within the budget that its starter grants. A recipe of the guest builder names up to 32 inputs, and the trees of those inputs give the files of its input disk.
