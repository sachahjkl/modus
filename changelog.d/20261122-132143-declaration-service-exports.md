**Exports of plans, recipes, projects, the guest builder and the collector**: a caller that acts for its principal now calls `plans.difference`, `plans.apply`, `plans.rollback` and `plans.generations` on its own plans, with a stale or unconsented apply and a rollback with no earlier generation refused by an error record; `recipes.register`, `recipes.build`, `recipes.cache_lookup`, `recipes.builds` and `recipes.realisations`, where a second build of a recipe comes from the cache and a recipe of the inputs tree builder builds in the service; `projects.load`, `projects.update`, which gives the one changed line of `pins.lex`, and `projects.pins`; `guest_builder.status` and `guest_builder.disks_check`; and the bounded spaces of the collector (`space_open`, `record_append`, `records_read`, `spaces`, `space_verify`, `source_count`, `record_count`), where a full audit space or a full forward queue refuses a record in place of a loss and a source never reads its own space.
