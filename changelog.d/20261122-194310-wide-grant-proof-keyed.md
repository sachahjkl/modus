**A wide-grant proof can no longer be forged.** The proof of a wide grant carries a keyed BLAKE3 tag under the key of the unlock authority, with its origin and a serial inside the tag, so a process without the key cannot make one, a changed field is refused, a proof from the remote display is refused, and one proof gives one grant. The collector refuses each wide grant when its start gave it no key, refuses an unknown act kind, sensitive action or revocation reason on its ring, and only the collector can clear the wide-grant mark of the compositor.
