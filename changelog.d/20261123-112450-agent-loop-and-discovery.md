**Several steps of an agent for one request**: the assistant answers a request in up to 16 steps of up to 16 acts each; it finds the commands of the system itself with `manual.modules`, `manual.exports` and `manual.signature`, reads each typed result, and goes on until its answer. It stops at 16 steps, at a step that repeats the acts and the results of the step before, or when you press Stop or Escape, and the window shows each step once, folds the results of older steps, and says once why the work ended. A request now holds 4096 bytes, a reply 16 KiB, and a table result carries 64 rows that the window pages through.
