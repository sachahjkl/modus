**The remote display.** The service `remote_display` sends the screen to a viewer on another machine over QUIC, as lossless tiles of the damage of the compositor, one frame in flight, with no pointer in the pixels; a viewer allowed for the input sends keys and a pointer, which take the path of the keys. A principal allows a key with `remote_display_allow(key, "view")` or `"view_and_input"`, `remote_disallow` ends the session at once, and the bar shows the mark `viewer` while a viewer is connected. A key allowed for the view alone makes no call of `remote`. The test part `remote_display` uses the peer of the tool as the viewer and compares the screen that it rebuilds with a picture of QEMU.
