**A remote display of a 4K, 5K or 8K screen.** A module states the pictures of the size of the screen that it holds, and the root service pays them at the size of the screen of the machine, so `remote_display` and the compositor follow a 4K, 5K or 8K screen as far as the share of the machine for a process holds; the screen of memory of a machine with no screen takes sizes up to 7680 × 4320.
