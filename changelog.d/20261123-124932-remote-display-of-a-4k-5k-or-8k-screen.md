**A remote display of a 4K, 5K or 8K screen.** A module states the pictures of the size of the screen that it holds, and the root service pays them at the size of the screen of the machine, so `remote_display` and the compositor follow a 4K, 5K or 8K screen as far as the share of the machine for a process holds. When the service cannot hold the screen in force, each viewer ends with the code `Screen_Not_Held` and one line counts them.
