**Comments in Lex**: `//` outside a text starts a comment to the end of the line, `area(r) // the area of r`. The console draws it in the color of a comment and runs the line before it. A line of only a comment, or of only spaces, now does nothing, where a line of spaces said that the line ends too soon. `//` inside a text stays a part of the text, and `a / / b` stays an error.
