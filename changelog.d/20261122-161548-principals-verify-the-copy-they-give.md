**The table that the principals service gives the root service is the table that it verified.** `principals` copies a disk grant table out of the call region of the storage service once, checks its tag on that copy, and gives the root service only that copy, so a storage service that changes the bytes after the check forges no row. `plans` and the root service stay on one generation of the roles of the machine after a lost answer, a late set or a failed compensation, and a `principals` that does not answer a call in time is stopped and started again.
