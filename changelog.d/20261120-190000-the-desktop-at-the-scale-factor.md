**The desktop at the scale factor.** The desktop of the screen of 1920 × 1080 is drawn at 1.25 by default, a logical screen of 1536 × 864, and at 1.375 with `settings.set(scale = .eleven_eighths)` or the new row of the settings window: the windows, the bar, the menus, the palette, the text and the pointer, each edge rounded to the nearest pixel of the screen; a scale from 1.5 to 2 is refused with `Out_Of_Range` until each window fits it. At a change of the scale each window lets its surface go and makes one at the scale that the compositor names, from its own budget; the compositor answers each request, a resize too (a refused one leaves the window at the size that it shows, with a line), never loses an answer, refuses a picture of a surface that it does not hold, and cuts each surface to its window, so no window draws over another between two scales. `Surface_Create` names the logical size and the scale of the surface, and a page that the read refuses keeps the scale in force. The pointer and the places of the compositor are logical pixels; a window that draws by hand shows a picture of its logical size; the text size of a draw record is at most 128 logical pixels (version 19, the exports `damage_of_draw_table_19` and `execute_draw_table_in_rectangles_19`); the window pages are counted at 1.375, and each module with a window states its window, so the least budget stays 2048 pages.
