**The command `Wire` and the wiring list of `Start`**: a process of the console or the start policy can ask the root service to join a record output of a process that it started, or of a service for which it acts for the principal, to a record input of another process or to itself; a `Start` from the console can carry the wiring of its child, which the root service applies whole before the first instruction of the child, or refuses whole. The root service checks that the two record descriptions are equal, pays the ring from the budget of the asker, ends each wiring with an end word at the stop of its writer, its reader or its asker, and records the wirings as the grant `wirings`, which `revoke` ends. Two records whose one field differs by its sign are no longer equal.
