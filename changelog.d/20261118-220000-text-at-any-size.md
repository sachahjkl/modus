**Text at any size.** Each process now draws the glyphs past its glyph caches at any text size, from a line of 4 to 256 pixels, into its own glyph cache of 1 MiB and 4 096 glyphs, with the pixels of today at the sizes of today: a host test compares each word of the 192 characters of each face at 16, 32 and 48 pixels with the glyph caches. A frame whose glyphs fill the cache still draws each glyph, and writes `text_rendering: the glyphs of one frame fill the private glyph cache` once on the log. The packages `fallback_glyphs` and `text_shaping` are one package, `text_rendering`, which also breaks the lines of a text in a box.
