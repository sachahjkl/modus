**The store of recipes keeps only what something names.** A failed guest build removes the objects that it wrote. A pass at the start of the service and after each guest build removes each object that no realisation, no recipe and no recent put names, and keeps each object under a named tree. A principal keeps at most 256 objects with `recipes.put` in one hour, whichever connection it uses. Objects larger than one call of the store, as a kernel or a large output file, are written and read in parts with one commit.
