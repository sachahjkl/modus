**A paint program.** `paint` has six tools (brush, eraser, line, rectangle, ellipse, fill), eight sizes, a palette of 16 colors and a custom color by channels, undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z, a status line, and pictures that it saves and opens in its private store. Its window is resizable, and a resize never cuts the picture. The exports `line` and `write` take the 16 colors of the palette, 0 to 15.
