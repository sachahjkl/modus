**A light browser window**: `start("browser")` opens a resizable window with back, forward, an address field and the document origin of the page; the pages built into it (modus://welcome, modus://properties, modus://guide, modus://forms) are HTML and CSS that the contract html_css parses, styles, lays out and paints into the draw table, with links, a history of 32 pages, a form whose values come back in the query, and a refused read of the state of another document origin. The window has no grant but its window: an address of HTTP or HTTPS shows that it has no network.
