**Every process says its first state of work, and the floors are measured.** Each process that the root service starts gets a ready ring at the handle 19 of its context, on which it says once that its start is done: the library says it at the first present of a window and at the first wait of a service, and the services and drivers before their main loop, so the machine test checks the floor of each of them. `modustool floors measured <log>` reads the peaks of a machine run and prints, for each module, the working memory that makes its floor its peak.
