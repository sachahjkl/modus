**unlock writes log facts**: each line of unlock is a record of fields, whose sentence is the line of before, the lines with no value too. A status of a kernel operation or of the root service is written by the name of its member ("status No_Right", where the line said "status 7"); an erase of versions that the store refused writes the fields of its error record ("kind Too_Long, unit Bytes, ..."), where the line wrote the sentence of the console. In the console, `log.records(0) |> facts(unlock.Passphrase_Wrong_Counted_By_Tpm) |> where(principal = 1)` gives each wrong passphrase of principal 1 that the TPM counted.
