**The demonstrations from imports to workers write log facts.** Each of their lines is now a record of fields, so `facts()` and `where()` in the console filter them by field: for example `facts(net.Round_Trip_Measured)`, `facts(kernel_events.Operations_Counted) |> where(verdict = "WRONG")`, `facts(scheduler.Strategy_Shares_Measured)`. The measure record, its page and the checks of a measure run read the sentence of a fact as they read a line. Most sentences stay as before; these changed. A status is written by its name where `locks`, `rights`, `tone` and the call host of `tables` wrote its number, a status of an isolated call of `tables` by its name too, and `tone` and `net` write the kind of an error record by its name. A share of `scheduler`, the times of a call and of a bind of `imports` and the rate of the generator of `randomness` have two decimals (`12.34 %`), in the log and in the window of `randomness`. A refusal of `net`, `notes` and `tables` writes its error record as its seven fields (`kind No_Grant, unit Endpoint, operation 2, ...`). A long line is several lines: each strategy of `scheduler` (its wakes, then its shares), its weight limit and the rounds under it, the counts of the server of `quic_echo`, the costs of the rules of `kernel_events`, the lock waits of `locks` (one line for each lock) and its frame caches (one line for each processor). A child of `kernel_events`, a process of the share policy of `scheduler` and the call host of `tables` that did not start write a fact of the status and of the budgets, then a fact of the stage of the load error, with the names and the hash heads of the modules that it concerns (`facts(tables.Call_Host_Load_Find_Failed)`).
