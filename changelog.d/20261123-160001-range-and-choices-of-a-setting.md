type: Changed

**The range and the choices of a setting in its description.** Module files are version 8: an Integer, a Handle or an Enum parameter can be optional, as a presence word then its value, and a parameter carries its range and the exports that give its choices and its instances. The console refuses a number out of its range before the call, and `compositor.set` takes the five settings of the display, the numbers included, in one call. Files of version 7 are refused.
