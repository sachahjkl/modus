**One test part from a range of its steps.** `modustool test main steps 1270 to 1280` boots the machine, runs its boot steps, the setup steps that each `with <first> to <last>` names and the steps of the range, then ends the part, with the time of each as a measure; `of <log name>` names another machine of the part. A range that names no step, ends before it starts or passes the steps of the machine is refused, and so is a step that needs a mark or the key of `remote` that no selected step gives, with the step to add.
