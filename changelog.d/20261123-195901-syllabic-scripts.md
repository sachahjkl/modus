**Khmer, Myanmar and the scripts of the Universal Shaping Engine.** A process with the script font set draws Khmer, Myanmar, Tibetan, Balinese, Javanese and Sundanese with their stacked letters, their vowel signs before the consonant and their marks in place, where it drew the replacement glyph. Each glyph, advance and offset of 154 test words of the six scripts is the one that HarfBuzz 12.3.0 gives on the same fonts. The script font set now holds 21 fonts.
