**The editor and the window of the debugger resize, on the layout tree.** The principal drags an edge of `edit` or of `debugger_window`, and each part follows: a top band with the fields and a state label, panes with a divider that a drag moves, a status band whose sentence is never cut, and the action bar. `edit` gets a scroll bar and a pane of its versions (Ctrl+H); `debugger_window` shows its threads and its variables in columns.
