**The screen, the proof and the mark of a wide grant.** `agent_allow(key, scopes, minutes)` shows the screen of a wide grant on the trusted display, where the principal picks the scopes, the windows and the duration, then types the passphrase in `unlock`; the bar shows the mark with the minutes left and revoke, and the keys and the pointer of a viewer reach only the windows picked. The grant ends at `agent_disallow`, at revoke, at its end time and at the lock.
