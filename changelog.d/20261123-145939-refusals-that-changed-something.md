**The refusals that changed something say so.** A command or a request that changed what its caller can see and then failed now ends with `Stopped_After_A_Partial_Change`: an extend of PCR 12 whose read back failed, a pin of the TPM after its index changed, a start whose wiring commit did not leave, a held debug start that stopped. A revocation of `Store` or `History` checks the room of each service ring before it changes anything. A foreign sealed object and a wrong passphrase at the TPM are `Argument_Refused` (`Not_Authentic`), a replacement row that does not verify is `Data_Damaged`, a locked session of a remote client is `Wrong_State`, and a refusal of the receiver of an isolated call names the unit `Region` or `Lent_Handles` with its amounts in bytes of the call region. The console, `unlock` and the test modules write these outcomes by their names.
