**The blocks that a wrong release keeps are freed on a disk larger than the memory.** A release or a dropped version that meets a wrong block now leaves a record of the leaves to recount, and the log says the blocks it keeps. The recount starts when free space falls under a sixteenth of the disk, or on `storage.recount()` from a caller that acts for a principal: one walk of the stores, during which reads are served and changes get `Busy` with a sentence that names the recount and its progress, then one commit for each directory of leaves that differ. A loss of power keeps the leaves still to recount.
