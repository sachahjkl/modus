**The blocks that a wrong release keeps are freed on a disk larger than the memory.** A release or a dropped version that meets a wrong block now leaves a record of the leaves to recount, and the log says the blocks it keeps. The recount starts when free space falls under a sixteenth of the disk, or on `storage.recount()` from the console, which holds the new start grant `Recount`; it starts only when the disk has room for its tally, and after a recount that found no room none starts until free space grows. It is one walk of the stores, with its names sorted by leaf, during which reads are served and changes get `Busy` with a sentence that names the recount and its progress, then one commit for each directory of leaves that differ. A loss of power keeps the leaves still to recount.
