**TLS 1.3 over TCP record layer and handshake library.** Added the freestanding zero-allocation `modus:tls_tcp` package implementing RFC 8446 record framing, SHA-256 HKDF key schedule, X25519 key exchange, Ed25519 `CertificateVerify`, and full client/server handshakes over `TLS_AES_128_GCM_SHA256` and `TLS_CHACHA20_POLY1305_SHA256` reusing `modus:cryptography_primitives`.
