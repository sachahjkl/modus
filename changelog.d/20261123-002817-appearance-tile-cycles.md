**The tile Appearance of the Control Center sets the next appearance**: a press goes auto, dark, light. With no window of settings open, the root service starts settings for one pass that keeps and writes the next appearance; with the window open, the press raises it with the focus on the appearance in force. Only the window that the root service started as settings takes the press, and while a pass of settings runs, the press and a start of the window are refused.
