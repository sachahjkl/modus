**Take the gold, leave the debt.** Property 25 is a rule of the system: modus takes the proven algorithms, structures, standards and lessons of other systems, leaves their compatibility layers, text formats and ambient rights, and wires each idea where its foundations give it more. Each reuse names its source and its first user, a rewritten algorithm keeps a reference, a fuzz test and a measure, code is reused as it is when a rewrite adds nothing, and an idea that contradicts another property stays out.
