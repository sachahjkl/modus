**Each angle is in turns.** The package `modus:support/trigonometry` gives the sine and the cosine of an angle in turns (1 is a whole circle, 0.25 a right angle), exact at each whole quarter turn, and each angle of the system, its guides and its site now counts in turns. `tone` and the sine table of `plasma` use it in place of a series in radians and an approximation of Bhaskara.
