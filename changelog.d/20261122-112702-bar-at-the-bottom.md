**The bar at the bottom of the screen**: `compositor.bar_position(2)` or the item "Bar Position Bottom" of the context menu of the desktop moves the whole bar to the bottom: the mark, the buttons `start` and `windows`, the hint of the palette, the clock, the mark of a viewer, the window plates and the tray badges. The start menu, the menu of the windows, the viewer list and the preview of a window plate open up from the bar; a maximized or snapped window, the notifications, the context menu and the trusted dialogs stay off the bar. A bar at the top keeps each place that it had.
