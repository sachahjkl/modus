**Each enum read from a word at its border**: an input record of the compositor or of the terminal, a status of the kernel or of the root service, a command of the service ring, a word of an error record, a kind of a value, of a code run, of a list icon, of a palette answer and a scope of a replacement row now name a member or are refused with a line of the log or an error record; no word that names no member is read as a member, and each switch on a closed set of the library names each member. The answers of the storage service are one enumeration with the same numbers, a place of a storage connection is its own type, `system.memory_size` gives the status of the kernel, and `ring_open` answers each cause with its own status.
