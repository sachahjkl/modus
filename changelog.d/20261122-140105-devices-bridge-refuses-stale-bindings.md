**The devices bridge refuses a stale or partial binding**: `devices` refuses a `Bind_Device` whose record names another device than the command, the recorded grant leaves out the MSI-X BARs as the real grant does, a 64-bit BAR past the page limit is refused rather than cut, only an identity with no class counts as an answer of a lookup, and a service that floods its ring no longer holds the wait of the root service.
