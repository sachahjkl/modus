**The cookies and the cache of the web client give each principal a part of its own.** The caller declares the principals that it serves, and each has a part of the memory of the size of its share, which the caller fixes (by default the room over those principals): a principal at its share replaces only its own least used cookie or cached answer, and one that fills its part never changes what another can hold or sees. A body past the share of a principal is refused before any entry goes.
