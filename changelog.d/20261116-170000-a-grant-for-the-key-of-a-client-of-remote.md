**A grant for the key of a client of `remote`.** A client of `remote` now proves an Ed25519 key of its own in the QUIC handshake (mutual TLS 1.3 with raw public keys), and `remote` takes only a key that a principal allowed with `remote_allow(key)` in a console that acts for it. Each call of that client acts for that principal, and for no other. A key that no principal allowed is refused in its handshake with access_denied, before any call, with one line of the log. `remote_disallow(key)` and a lock of the session of the principal end the grant: the root service ends the connections to services of that key, and `remote` ends the open connection of the client with the code Revoked. `remote_clients()` lists the keys of a principal; one principal allows 4 keys at most, of the 8 of the machine. Before, `remote` admitted the host by its address alone, and each program of the host called as the principal of the session of `remote`. `modustool run` prints the key of the client of its peer, which the console allows. A grant lasts until the machine stops.
