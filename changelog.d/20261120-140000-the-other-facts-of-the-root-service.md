**The other lines of the root service are log facts.** The root service writes each of its lines as a log fact, the lines with no value too: the budgets and the mappings of a start, the devices, the debugging, the resources, the replacements, the refusals of a start from the disk, the disk grants of a nickname, the disk grant tables, the client keys of remote, the principals, the trust of the boot and the start policy, so a reader filters them by field, as `log.records(0) |> facts(root.Table_Written) |> where(principal = 0)`. A status of the kernel names its member ("status No_Memory", not "status 7"), the refusal of the entropy service, of the network service and of the storage service gives the fields of its error record, the machine parts page writes one line for each part, and a load that the loader refuses gives a line of the start that failed, then a fact of the fields of its stage (`root.Load_Find_Failed` and the others), in place of the sentence of the loader. No line of the root service is text any more.
