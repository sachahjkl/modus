**The windows of the system write log facts.** The compositor, the console, the terminal, `edit`, `manual`, `settings`, `inspect`, `debugger_window`, `monitor` and `log` write each of their lines as a log fact, and a reader filters them by field, as `log.records(0) |> facts(settings_process.Values_Changed) |> where(font = "spleen")`. Most sentences stay as they were; a line of the console, a debug place, a link of the manual and the text of a field of the palette are text views, whole up to the area of a fact. A status is written by the name of its member ("status Bad_Handle"), an error record by its fields ("kind Too_Long, unit Bytes, operation 3, ..."), the problem of a pointer theme file by the names of its part, its field and its rule, the menu of the windows as one line for each window, the list of the palette as its count and one line for each of its first rows, and the stop that `inspect` shows as one line for the fault and one for each frame of the stack.
