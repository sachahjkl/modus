**The windows of the system write log facts.** The compositor, the console, the terminal, `edit`, `manual`, `settings`, `inspect`, `debugger_window`, `monitor` and `log` write their lines as log facts, a reader filters them by field, as `log.records(0) |> facts(settings_process.Values_Changed) |> where(font = "spleen")`, and most sentences stay as they were. A status is written by the name of its member ("status Bad_Handle"), an error record of the storage service or of the root service by its fields ("kind Too_Long, unit Bytes, operation 3, ..."), the menu of the windows as one line for each window ("compositor: windows: console, the window 1 of 3"), and the list of the palette as its count and one line for each of its first rows. A line that holds a line of the console, a debug place, a link of the manual or a text of a field of the palette, which can pass the 144 bytes of the values of a fact, stays text.
