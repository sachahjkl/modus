**The windows of the system write log facts.** The compositor, the console, the terminal, `edit`, `manual`, `settings`, `inspect`, `debugger_window`, `monitor` and `log` write each line that has a value as a log fact: the moves and the sizes of a window, the clipboard, the palette, the pointer theme, the history and the palette table of the console, the keys and the scroll bars of the terminal, the versions of `edit`, the samples of `inspect`, the threads and the frames of the debugger, the settings in force. Each sentence is the line of before, and a reader filters them by field, as `log.records(0) |> facts(settings_process.Values_Changed) |> where(font = "spleen")`. A line with no value, a time with one decimal, a measure that the measure record reads, and a text with no bound below the values of one record stay text.
