**A font for the missing Greek letters, Cyrillic and Latin Extended-A.** Noto Sans, as the subset "Noto Sans LGC" (87 172 bytes), is the last font of each fallback chain: every window and every cell draws the 49 Greek letters (Π and ρ too), the 96 basic Cyrillic letters (U+0400 to U+045F) and the 128 letters of Latin Extended-A (U+0100 to U+017F), and the compositor names no Greek or Cyrillic letter as missing on its log. A bold face draws these letters in the regular weight of Noto Sans.
