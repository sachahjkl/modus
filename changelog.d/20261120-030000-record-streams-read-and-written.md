**A process writes and reads its record streams**: `system.output_write` writes one record into the ring of each reader, whole or refused, `system.output_end` ends the stream, and `system.input_read` gives the next record, the joined record, a gap record or the end, with the end word of a writer that stopped; each reader checks each record against a description file that the root service gives it. The module format is version 5: each record output and record input names the place of its variable, where each loader writes the index of its row. The machine test `output_wiring` checks each refusal of a wiring with the budget of its asker unchanged, the joined record at 0 and at 5, and the end words of a writer, a reader and an asker that stop and of a revocation.
