**Sound, a timed stream.** A program with the start grant `Sound_Device` plays sound through the new sound service `sound`, the device service of a virtio sound device: it writes its samples in memory of its own, lends it with the right Read only, and sends records that each name the time of their first frame by the system clock. The service plays each frame at its time, 48000 frames in one second of two channels of signed 16-bit samples, with silence between the records and a latency of 30 to 40 ms. A record that comes late, more than 10 s ahead, over the record before it, of an unknown format or past its region is refused whole with its error record, and one program plays at a time: a second one does not start. The program `tone` plays a tone of 440 Hz, and `modustool test sound` finds it in the sound output of the machine at the frame of its time. The error records gain the units `Nanoseconds` and `Frames`.
