**A module declares its record outputs and record inputs**: a package variable `requests: system.Record_Output(Access_Record)`, `orders: system.Record_Input(Order, .Many)` or `source: system.Record_Input_With_Terms(system.Any_Record, Condition)` becomes a row of the new tables `Record_Outputs` and `Record_Inputs` of the module file (format version 4), with its record description, its line of help, its pairing or its count of wirings, and its wiring terms; `system.Any_Record` declares a generic one. The build refuses a declaration that is not a package variable of the module, a record with padding or a Handle field, two streams with one name, and a generic record output with no generic record input. The contract package `output` gives the compartments of an output ring and the border that checks each one; no process writes a record output yet.
