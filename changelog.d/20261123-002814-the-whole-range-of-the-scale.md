**The scales from 1.5 to 2.** The principal sets each scale from 1 to 2 by eighths: the track of the window of settings has nine stops, 100 % to 200 %, each selectable, and settings.set takes each member. The budget of a window counts its pictures at 2 (a window of 1000 x 704 logical pixels holds 5500 pages in place of 2600 at 1.375). A window whose budget still cannot pay a scale declines it and shows its frame only, in place of a stop of its process, and the window of settings says how many did; it offers a surface again only at another scale, and writes one line for each scale that it declines. Before a new scale, each window is fitted into the new work area: a maximized window stays maximized, a larger window gets the size that fits, and each window stays on the screen, so a terminal maximized at 1.25 shows its surface at 2. A maximize and a snap take the work area of the scale in force, not the one of the scale at the start.
