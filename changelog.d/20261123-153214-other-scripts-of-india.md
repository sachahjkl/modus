**The shaping of the other scripts of India**: a process with the script font set draws Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam as they are written, each with its Noto font, on the shaper of Devanagari: the conjuncts, the below and post forms, the reph at the place of its script, the vowel signs of two parts, the pre-base forms of ra. Each glyph, advance and offset of 142 test words is that of HarfBuzz on the same fonts. The script font set holds 15 fonts, 40 030 968 bytes.
