**Swiss tables and rapidhash V3 for in-memory lookups.** The support packages `modus:support/hash_table` (`Hash_Table` and `Hash_Index`, 16-slot groups with SSE2 tag matching, 7/8 load bound, in-place tombstone rebuild, and seeded hashing) and `modus:support/fast_hash` (rapidhash V3, keeping BLAKE3 for identity and security) give modules fixed-capacity, zero-allocation tables for outside keys. On the host (`-o:speed -microarch:x86-64`, load 3.71), random-key lookups measure 1.46 ns hit / 1.29 ns miss at 16 entries, 2.21 / 1.82 ns at 1 000 entries, and 4.11 / 5.88 ns at 100 000 entries, and rapidhash V3 hashes 8 B and 16 B keys in 2.68 ns, 32 B in 3.71 ns, and 64 B in 6.19 ns. The private glyph cache (`text_rendering.Private_Cache`) now uses `hash_table.Hash_Index` in place of its chained bucket table, keeping the same 354-page glyph memory budget.
