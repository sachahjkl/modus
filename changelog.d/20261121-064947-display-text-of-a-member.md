**The display text of a member, apart from its name**: a module file (version 7) holds a table of display texts by member and language tag of BCP 47 (`en`, `fr-CA`), which a module declares in its source apart from the enum: `@(member_texts = "en")`. A reader follows the tags of the principal as the Lookup of RFC 4647 does (`fr-CA`, `fr`), then `en`, then the name of the member. The palette shows "settings.set scale to 1.75" and the button "1.75" for the scale `.fourteen_eighths`, the completion lists `.fourteen_eighths   1.75`, and a line still types the name. The settings give texts for the scale, the subpixel order (`RGB`, `BGR`), the pointer theme and the keyboard layout (`US`). A row that names no member, a text past 24 bytes, a text with a character that a reader does not see or that changes the order of the text, a text of spaces only, a tag that is not well-formed or not in its canonical case, and two texts for one member and one tag are refused, and the build refuses a row of the palette that would miss a button.
