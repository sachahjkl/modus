**The context menu of the desktop takes the pointer and shows its keys**: the line under the pointer is chosen, a press on it acts, and a press outside closes the menu. Each line shows at its right the keys that do the same act (Ctrl+Shift+M for the magnifier), the new items snap the window in front to the left or the right half or maximize it, and the item of the edge that the bar holds now is disabled. The card has the gradient of the cards with a line of light, the input line of the interface as its filter, and a line under each matched character.
