**The audit of the collector proved on the machine.** The machine test `persist` starts the collector and a source with a list whose row pairs `collector.forward` with that source: each record comes to its reader through the pair, in its order, after the audit space kept it, and both audit spaces and their hash chains are whole after a stop of the collector and after a restart of the machine. A pair with no source row and a second pair of one source are refused.
