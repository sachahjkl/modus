**The pairs of the collector, and its store**: a wiring list gives `collector.forward` one ring for each source that asks to forward, named by the new field `paired_row` of its row, and the collector keeps each record of a source in its audit space, in its private store, before the ring of its pair receives it; a full space or a full ring holds the writer, and no record is lost. A pair has the pace `Always_Holds_Writer`, and a consumer that does not read, or a pair that never comes, holds its own source only, never the forward of the other sources. The collector starts on demand with the grants `Serve` and `Store`, so its audit spaces live in a store on the machine.
