**The guest builder.** `recipes.build` of a recipe whose builder is the guest builder packs its inputs into a read-only disk, runs the command of its spec in a Linux guest with a blank output disk, no network and a frozen time, and keeps the tree of the output disk as the realisation of the recipe. A build is refused, with nothing kept, when the guest exits with a status that is not 0, writes a damaged or oversize output disk, writes to its input disk or reaches the bound of its exits. The new export `recipes.put` keeps a blob or a tree by its hash, so a recipe names it as an input.
