**The call outcome of a role call between machines.** A role call through the proxy between paired machines, and an outbound request of `remote`, now end with the same call outcome as a call on one machine: the holder gives its outcome, which crosses the link, and a broken link or a passed bound gives an outcome in doubt, so a client of a remote role tells a refusal from a call in doubt as it does for a local holder.
