**A builder started in its sandbox.** `recipes.build` now runs a builder module named by its hash in the recipe. The service `recipes` starts it as a call host with a read-only input store that holds only the objects of its inputs, an empty store "out", no network and no root ring, and a seed equal to the recipe hash. The output hash is the tree hash of the store "out". A builder that writes its inputs, fails, stops, or runs past the bound of 60 s is refused, and nothing is kept. A builder that is no module of the boot image is refused with `Builder_Absent`, and an input that no build gave with `Input_Absent`. A share of the storage service now gives its handles with the right Give, so its maker can pass them to another process.
