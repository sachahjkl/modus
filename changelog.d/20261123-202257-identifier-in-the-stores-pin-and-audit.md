**The principal identifier in the stores, the pin and the audit**: each store of a named principal names its owner by its principal identifier in its store block, and keeps only a name tag in its cell, so a reader of the disk reads no name of it; the pin of the file of a principal and the index of its collector name the identifier too. A data disk of an older release is refused with "the data disk has the format of an older release of modus, which this release does not read: write the data disk again", and the audit spaces of an older collector with a sentence of their own.
