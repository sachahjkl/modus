A process with the script font set draws Khmer, Myanmar, Tibetan, Balinese, Javanese and Sundanese: ខ្មែរ, မြန်မာ, བོད་, ᬩᬮᬶ, ꦗꦮ and ᮞᮥᮔ᮪ᮓ draw with their stacked letters, their vowel signs before the consonant and their marks in place.

Khmer and Myanmar each take a shaper of their own model; Tibetan, Balinese, Javanese and Sundanese take the Universal Shaping Engine. Each finds a syllable by the grammar that HarfBuzz uses. Each glyph, advance and offset of 154 test words of the six scripts is the one that HarfBuzz gives on the same fonts.

The script font set now holds 21 fonts, 43 294 696 bytes of fonts.
