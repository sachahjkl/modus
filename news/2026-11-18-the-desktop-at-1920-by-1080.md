modus is an operating system with a small kernel, in which each program draws its window into memory of its own, and the compositor shows it.

The test machines now show the reference screen of the system, 1920 by 1080 pixels, in place of 1024 by 768. Each picture of the gallery is a picture of that screen. The compositor shows each frame whole: it writes the frame into one of two screen pages in the video memory, then makes the display show that page. Two pages of 1920 by 1080 take 16 588 800 of the 16 777 216 bytes of the virtual display.

The windows keep their sizes for now, so they take a smaller part of the screen. Next, a scale factor from 1.25 to 2 draws them, their text and the pointer larger, on the same screen.
