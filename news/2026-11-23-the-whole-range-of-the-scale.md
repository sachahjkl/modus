The principal sets the scale of the desktop to each eighth from 1 to 2: the track of the window of settings has nine stops, from 100 % to 200 %, and each stop changes the whole desktop at once.

At 1 a logical pixel is a pixel of the screen, 1920 x 1080. At 2 the logical screen is 960 x 540: a window taller than the work area keeps its title on the screen, and the principal moves it to see the rest.

The budget of each window counts its pictures at 2, so each window pays its surface at each scale. A window whose budget still cannot pay a scale shows its frame only until another scale, and the window of settings says how many do.

The tile Appearance of the Control Center now sets the next appearance: auto, dark, light.
