title: A device reaches only the memory of its service
date: 2026-11-14

The kernel programs the IOMMU of Intel when the firmware lists one. A device then reads and writes only the pages that its device service declared, at the moment it asked the kernel for their physical address. A device service with a fault can no longer make its device write the memory of another program. An access to another page is refused: the kernel writes one line on the log with the device, the address and the count of such faults, and no program stops. The test machine with the IOMMU starts the whole system with translation on. Then a test device service reads a block of the disk into a page that it did not declare: the page keeps its bytes, the line of the fault comes, and the service still answers. A machine with no IOMMU writes one line at its start and runs as before. The IOMMU of AMD and interrupt remapping are not built.
