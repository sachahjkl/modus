Alt+Tab opens a card of the windows in the middle of the screen, the one used last first, and chooses the window used before the one in front. Each Tab chooses the next one, and Alt+Shift+Tab goes backward. The release of Alt brings the chosen window to the front. Escape closes the card, and no key reaches a window while it is open.

The plates of the bar now keep the order in which the windows opened. A press on the plate of the window in front minimizes it; a press on another plate brings its window to the front. The right button on a plate opens the menu of its window.
