With the setting `keyboard_layout` on `french`, AltGr, the right Alt key, gives the third level of the keys of the row of the digits: `#`, `{`, `[`, `|`, `\`, `^`, `@`, `]` and `}`. So the whole console language is typed with that layout. AltGr with the key of 2 is the dead tilde, and with the key of 7 the dead grave accent: `~` then `n` types ñ, and `` ` `` then Shift `a` types À. A space after them types the accent alone. AltGr with `e` types no euro sign yet, because the fonts do not draw it.
