With the setting `keyboard_layout` on `french`, the key of 2 types é, and the other keys of the row of the digits type è, ç and à. The key right of P is a dead key: `^` then `e` types ê, and with Shift it gives the diaeresis of ë, ï and ü. The console keeps the line in UTF-8, and Left, Right and Backspace move over or remove a whole character. Each font of the system draws the 96 characters of the Latin-1 Supplement, so é shows as a letter and not as a box. A passphrase can hold them too.
