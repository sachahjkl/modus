A process with the script font set draws Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam: বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ and മലയാളം draw with their conjuncts, their below and post forms and their reph.

One shaper serves the nine scripts, with one row for each: its virama, its ra, the place of its reph. Each glyph, advance and offset of 176 test words of the nine scripts is the one that HarfBuzz gives on the same fonts.

The script font set now holds 15 fonts, 40 030 968 bytes.
