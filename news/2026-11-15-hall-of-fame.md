The program hall_of_fame shows the people whose work inspired modus: the language that it is written in, the emulator that runs its tests, the immediate-mode interface, game engines, browser engines, and streams that build programs from scratch. It replaces the temple of Jon, which showed one turning portrait.

Each person has a card with the name and one line on what they made. Five cards have a portrait, with the author and the license of the picture under it. A person with no picture under a free license has a card with no picture: the system invents no face.

The arrows, Home, End and the pointer choose a card. The view moves by whole rows, so no card and no text is cut at its edge. The page of the licenses of the site lists each portrait with its source.
