The window of `settings` shows four sections: Display, Pointer, Keyboard, and Region and language. Each setting has one line that says what it does, and the value in force is the marked button.

The scale is a track of seven stops, from 125 % to 200 %. The stops from 150 % are dim: they wait for windows that fit a smaller logical screen. A press on a stop changes the scale of the whole desktop at once.

The time zone is a list of the 38 offsets that the earth uses, each with places that keep it as their standard time. The languages are a list of names in their order, with Up and Remove.

The window resizes from 400 x 320 to 1024 x 800 logical pixels. Its sections take one column, or two from a width of 720, and a scroll bar moves them when they do not fit.

A press now reaches the element under the pointer at each scale: the window drew its frame with no scale, and at 1.25 a press on one button chose another.
