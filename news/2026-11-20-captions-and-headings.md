modus is an operating system with a small kernel, in which each program draws its window into memory of its own, and the compositor shows it.

The elements of a window now take the text sizes of the design rules. The caption of a card is at 14 pixels, on the baseline of its title. The one number of a card is at 32 pixels, and what it counts is a caption under it. Each page of the manual has its title at 24 pixels over its text, and the title stays when the text moves. The result of the palette stays at 48 pixels, and each other text at 16.

The card "THE TEXT OF THE SYSTEM" of the window `settings` shows each size, one under the other. A caption keeps a contrast of 4.5 to 1 on a card in the dark and in the light appearance.
