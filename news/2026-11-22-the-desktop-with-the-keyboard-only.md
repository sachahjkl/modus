Control+Shift+F7 moves the window in front with the arrow keys, 16 pixels at each key or 1 with Shift. Control+Shift+F8 resizes it the same way: an outline shows the new size, and Enter asks its process for it. Escape gives back the old place and the old size. The palette offers both as "move window" and "resize window".

Control+Shift+F10 gives the bar the focus of the keys. Left and Right go from the heron button to the button of the windows and to each window plate. Down opens a menu, Up and Down walk its lines, Enter starts the module or brings the window to the front, and Escape returns to the window.

The thing with the focus has an outline of 2 pixels in the strong text, outside it: a button, a field or a list of a window, an item of the bar, a window that the arrows hold. A host test computes its contrast on each surface: 7.3 to 1 at the least, in the dark and the light appearance and in both high contrast sets.
