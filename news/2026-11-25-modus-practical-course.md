A hands-on practical course now explains the core properties of `modus` — what they are, how they work internally, and how to use them daily — both as a six-lesson written guide (`docs/COURSE.md`) and as a five-lesson video with two-panel syntax-highlighted teaching slides (`modustool film course`).

The course walks in plain English through typed `processes()` tables, `memory()` records, exact unit arithmetic (`1 MiB + 512 KiB`), pure Lex `filter`/`map`, closed `.enum` choices, the execution modes (`start`, `start_from_disk`, `connect`, `isolate`, `load`) with live `help("readings")` inspection, declarative `plan { ... }` execution with trusted display consent, live capability inspection in the `grants` window, automatic copy-on-write version preview and restore in `notes`, and one-step `projects.install` alongside the four `recipes.*` cache steps.
