The pages Releases and News of the site now have a button "Subscribe" under their heading. It opens a small menu with two links: the Atom feed of the page, and the releases of the repository on GitHub, where the button "Watch" sends a notification at each release.

The menu opens and closes with no script, with the pointer or with Enter. With the script, Escape or a click outside closes it. The menu is as wide as its longest line, so no label is cut.
